Why swallow it

A serum stops
at the surface.

Reasonable question: if it's for your skin, why not put it on your skin? The short answer is that the layer you're trying to reach isn't the one a cream lands on.

Topicals work top-down

Creams and serums are formulated to act on the epidermis — the outermost layer. That's genuinely useful for texture and hydration, and it's why you shouldn't stop using them.

Skin is fed from underneath

New skin cells are built in the dermis and pushed upward over roughly a month. Whatever's circulating in your blood while that happens is part of the raw material.

So the two aren't rivals

Viterglow isn't a replacement for your routine. It's the input side of it — which is also why it takes a full turnover cycle before there's anything to judge.

Questions

Before you order.

How many capsules do I take?

One capsule a day for adults, with water, as printed on the box. Each box holds 30 capsules. Taking more than the stated dose won't speed anything up.

Is it Halal?

Yes — Viterglow is certified Halal by BPJPH Indonesia. Note that the capsule shell is bovine gelatin, so it isn't suitable for vegetarians or vegans.

Is it registered with BPOM?

Yes. The registration number is POM SD 243 013 421, printed on the box next to the Halal mark. Viterglow is manufactured by PT Harbat Farma in Tangerang.

Why is the capsule pink?

Partly the astaxanthin, which is naturally deep red, and partly the capsule shell colouring — FD&C Red no.40, D&C Red no.28, FD&C Blue no.1 and titanium dioxide. All of it is listed on the back of the box.

Can I take it with other supplements or medication?

Usually, but we can't advise on your particular situation. If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, taking prescription medication or managing a health condition, speak to a doctor or pharmacist first.
